LMS XII

GAME RULES

Scoring

4 points for a player goal, 2 points for an assist.

Home win against a level team gives 60 points. Adjusted for goal/assist combinations.

Team Picks

Select your team for the week carefully. Points are awarded based on fixtures.

Player Picks

You may select a player per week. Once a player is picked, they cannot be picked again for that week.

Other Rules

Draws are scored at 30% of a full win. Goal: 30 points, Assist: 15 points. Ensure selections are entered before submitting.
